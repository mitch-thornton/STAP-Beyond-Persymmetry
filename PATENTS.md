# Patents

The MIT license in this repository grants copyright permissions only. It does
not grant any rights under patents.

- A graduate student studying the method for research and learning needs no
  patent license.
- A researcher comparing against the method in an academic publication needs no
  patent license.
- A company deploying the method in a commercial product may need a patent
  license.
- Downstream open-source redistribution does not remove the need for a separate
  patent license for commercial deployment.

U.S. patents pending, assigned to Clearpoint Research, LLC. The method in this
repository is covered by:

- U.S. Provisional Patent Application No. 64/084,675, covering group-averaged
  covariance estimation for space-time adaptive processing: the estimate as a
  Reynolds projection onto the commutant of a finite symmetry group, the
  effective dimension as a group gain computable before data collection, the
  selection of the matched group, the diagonalized inversion, and the convex
  combination of the sample covariance with the group average at a closed-form
  intensity.
- U.S. Provisional Patent Application No. 64/080,183, covering sensor array
  configuration by group-theoretic criteria, which is relevant to the reading of
  the effective dimension as a figure of merit for ranking array geometries.
